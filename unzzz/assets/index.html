        Special days

        Import a year of working and rest dates for school terms, shift patterns or any non-standard week. A date marked “work” rings even on a Sunday; a date marked “holiday” stays quiet even on a Monday.

        Format: date,type,note — for example 2026-01-01,holiday,New Year or 2026-01-04,work,Make-up workday. Use work/working for a working day and holiday/rest for a rest day. A header row is optional.
